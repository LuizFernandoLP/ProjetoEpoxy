How to use

  1. Start the rear camera.
  2. Tap Auto-detect floor and wait for the AI model to load (first load may take a minute).
  3. Move the camera slowly while keeping the floor visible; segmentation refreshes about once per second.
  4. Or choose Manual corners and tap the four floor corners.
  5. Choose a preset or upload a texture, then adjust strength, gloss, and scale.

AI mode: uses an on-device browser segmentation model after it downloads; performance varies by phone and lighting. It refreshes the visible floor mask, but is not true AR/world-locked tracking. Manual corners stay fixed to the camera view. Keep people, furniture, and walls out of the main view when possible.

Preview only. Actual epoxy color and reflectivity vary with lighting, preparation, and application.
